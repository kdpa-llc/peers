# Calm technology (clipped)

Technology should require the smallest possible amount of attention. It can communicate, but it does not need to speak. It should inform and create calm.

Principles worth keeping:

1. Make use of the periphery.
2. Amplify the best of technology and the best of people.
3. Work even when it fails.
