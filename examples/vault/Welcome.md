# Welcome to your vault

Bots write the pages here, and you steer them. Every project and every area has its own bot.

## How to steer

- **Select text** and type what should change.
- **Circle, box or point** at anything with the tools at the bottom of the page.
- Queue several notes, then press **Send** so the owning bot handles them in one go.
- **Chat** with the bot on the right. It follows the page you have open.
- Every bot edit can be undone, and every page has history.

## Where things live

| Folder | What goes there |
| --- | --- |
| Projects | Work with a goal and an end date |
| Areas | Parts of life you keep up over time |
| Resources | Reference material |
| Archive | Finished or paused projects and areas |
| Inbox | Anything not sorted yet |
